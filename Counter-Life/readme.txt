20th November 2004 Beta 6.5
Reorganised buy menus to follow CS 1.6 layout.
Rebalanced weapon value system based on effectiveness vs. enemy AI.
Rebalanced money received from killing enemy AI based on enemy strength.

New dynamic lighting effects have been added to all player and enemy AI weapons.
New dynamic blast physics have been added to all explosive types varying in degree and radius.
New dynamic smoke puff chamber discharges occur everytime a bullet is fired from a players weapon.
New high-velocity bouncing shrapnel have been added to all explosive types varying in count and damage.
New realisticly scaled shell casing models for each round type based on calibre.

Realistic player movement physics.
Realistic player weapon weight and handling physics.
Realistic locational damage system for both Player and enemy AI.
Realistic bullet damage adjustment system vs. body armor based on calibre and penetrating power.
Realistic ROF, specs, recoil and general operation accurate to real-world counter-part weapons.
Realistic player weapon carrying capacity.
Realistic player ammo carrying capacity.
Realistic player movement speed.

Weapons with mounted scopes retain their unzoomed ROF.
Assault Rifles with mounted scopes now have a standard 1.5x/3x zoom ratio.
New single shot disposable RPG Launcher added to player aresenal.
New remote detonated C4 shape charge added to player aresenal.
New Dual USP .45 pistol(s) added to player aresenal.
New Dual Desert Eagle .50AE pistol(s) added to player aresenal.
All Turrets now eject shell casings when fired.
World shell casing model "time to fade" has been doubled for emersive realism.

The Benelli M3 Super 90 is now chambered for the 12 guage armor penetrating solid slug.
The Benelli M4 Super 90 is now chambered for the 12 guage magnum buckshot shell.
The P228 has been corrected and is now chambered for the 9x19mm Luger/Para cartridge.

Benelli M4 Super 90 is now Semi-Automatic

New hazard course firing range and training facility map by Moistnote.

-=[ Game Information ]=-

COMMANDS:
  buy		 Calls up the buy menu (note: longjump and knife required)
  drop           Drops your active weapon
  bank           Displays your current amount of money
  money          Money cheat
  terra          Gives HCBV, Knife and manximum amount of money obtainable
 
VARIABLES:
  cl_buytime     Delay before buy menu will disappear
  cl_maxmoney    Maximum amount of money obtainable (limited to: 950,000,000)
  cl_startmoney  Amount of money you will own at the beginning of a game (limited to: 950,000,000)
  cl_getcash     Toggles whether you receive money directely for killing monsters
  cl_white       Enables white HUD weapon slots so that colored ones looks better
  cl_left	 Switches bullet ejection and smokepuffs to co-ordinate with the 
                 left or right handed models
  mp_buytime     Delay before buy menu will disappear (in multiplayer)
  mp_maxmoney    Maximum amount of money obtainable (in multiplayer)
  mp_startmoney  Amount of money you will own at the beginning of a game (in multiplayer)

WEAPON LIMITATIONS:
  Knifes: 1
  Handguns: 1
  Shotguns: 1
  Sub-Machine guns: 1
  Rifles: 2 (1 assault, 1 sniper)
  Machine guns: 1
  LAW : 1
  C4 : 3
  HE Grenades: 10
  Flashbangs: 0 (redundant bastards)

OBTAINING WEAPONS:

On Earth:
  First get the HCBV suit and find the knife. Use any Health Stations or HEV stations you see to buy your
  arsenal.

In Xen:
  Get the longjump module and then press the key binded to the 'buy' command.

MAIN BUY MENU:
 1. Handguns
 2. Shotguns
 3. Sub-Machine Guns
 4. Rifles ---------------------- 1. Assault Rifles --- 2. Sniper Rifles
 5. Heavy Weapons

 6. Equipment
 7. Ammo

 0. Exit

MONEY:
  There are 2 ways to earn money and both are dependant on the CVAR command 'cl_getmoney'
  If it's set to '1', then you will need to kill enemies to earn cash.
  If it's set to '0', then the weapons in Half-Life will be replaced by wads of cash.
  
  Original Half-life weapons which are scattered about in the whole game are now replaced by wads of cash regardless
  of 'cl_getmoney'. The only thing that the command affects is the amount you get.
